FAQ

We offer worldwide shipping with DHL Express and UPS. Orders within Germany take 1–3 business days, while international deliveries take 3–7 business days. Free shipping is available on all orders over 100€.
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Klarna. All payments are processed securely with SSL encryption.
You can return unworn items with original tags attached within 14 days of delivery. Contact info@veyastudio.com with your order number to receive your return label.
Orders can be canceled within 2 hours after placement before dispatch. Please reach out to info@veyastudio.com immediately if you wish to modify or cancel your order.
